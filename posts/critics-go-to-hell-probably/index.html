announcing book two - critics go to hell probably 🔥

Posted on Nov 16, 2026
tl;dr: we've printed off the white pube website

in 2015 i started a website called the white pube with my art sister Zarina Muhammad. we began posting texts every sunday about art, mostly reviews, sometimes essays, and then sometimes not even about art. theme parks, video games, restaurants, books. In February, Penguin imprint Particular are publishing a collection of that sunday writing alongside a conversation between me and Zarina about whether we think we are going to hell for being critics (probably)

feeling good! As I hunker down to finish book three, a fiction book I’m doing solo, it feels like this has to come first. this decade of writing where I went from baby art student to relentless imp. haha. i love being a critic

Josephine Greywoode was our editor. And Tom Etherington did this cover too! I can’t wait to see it on the shelf next to Poor Artists.
