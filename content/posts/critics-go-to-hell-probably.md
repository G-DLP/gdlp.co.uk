in 2015 i started a website called the white pube with my art sister Zarina Muhammad. we began posting texts every sunday about art, mostly reviews, sometimes essays, and then sometimes not even about art. theme parks, video games, restaurants, books. In February, Penguin imprint Particular are publishing a collection of that sunday writing alongside a conversation between me and Zarina about whether we think we are going to hell for being critics (probably)
